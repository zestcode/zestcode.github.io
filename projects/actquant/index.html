ActQuant: Sub-4-bit Action-Guided Quantization for Vision-Language-Action Models

Coauthored research on action-guided mixed-precision quantization for deploying vision-language-action models with less memory.

VLAQuantizationModel efficiencyUR3

Overview

Action-guided quantization allocates precision to the parts of a model that matter for control, making compact VLA deployment a research question about behavior as well as memory.

My contribution

Coauthor. The paper studies sub-4-bit VLA quantization, with simulation and real-robot evaluation. See the paper for the full method, author list, and experimental protocol.

  • Action-guided bit allocation and quantization-scale optimization
  • Evaluation in simulation and on a physical UR3 arm
