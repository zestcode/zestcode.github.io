Overview

Wrist-mounted cameras encounter motion and visual conditions that differ from conventional navigation. This work evaluates localization in the setting where a robot interacts with objects.

My contribution

Evaluating ORB-SLAM3 and VGGT-SLAM on manipulation-specific camera motion, with stereo and stereo-inertial trajectory error as quantitative evidence.

  • Wrist-camera evaluation for robot manipulation
  • ORB-SLAM3 and VGGT-SLAM
  • Stereo and visual-inertial state estimation
